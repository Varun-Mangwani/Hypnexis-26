Cohort Retention & Customer Economics Overview

Cohort Metrics Analysis:
Overall gross revenue retention across all mid-market cohorts averaged 91.2%. Customers under 100 seats experienced 88% retention, while upper mid-market accounts (100 to 500 seats) demonstrated 93.5% retention.
For strategic enterprise accounts exceeding 500 seats, gross revenue retention reached 96.8% with net dollar expansion of 124%.

Acquisition & Unit Economics:
Customer acquisition costs (CAC) decreased by 8% in the enterprise tier during Q2 and Q3 due to improved organic inbound conversion from our open-source evaluation benchmarks. Payback period for enterprise deals shortened from 14.2 months to 11.8 months.

Churn Analysis:
Reasons for mid-market churn:
1. Consolidation into existing legacy suites (42% of churned ARR)
2. Budget reductions following macroeconomic pressures (31%)
3. Insufficient in-house ML expertise to manage custom RAG deployments (27%)
